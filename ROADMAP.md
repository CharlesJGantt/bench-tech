# Bench Tech roadmap

## ✅ Milestone 1 — Is diagnosing fun? (done)
One board (Brightline DL-12 desk lamp, ~40 parts, 5 blocks), circuit sim, 22 tiered fault variants across the 10 prototype families, multimeter (V/Ω/continuity/diode), bench PSU (jack or clip-lead injection, current limit, CC), customer adapter, finger test, IPA drying on hot parts, magnified inspection, soldering minigame (flux, cold joints, lifted pads), jumper wires, parts drawer, customer test + debrief, comebacks, cash/reputation, guided mode.

## Milestone 2 — Hands-on device
- Lamp enclosure in Blender: screws, base cover, ribbon/JST cable to the lamp head
- Disassembly / reassembly (screw tray, leftover screw problem), cable latch damage
- Real-time play-test pass: tune difficulty, hint wording, payouts

## ✅ Milestone 3a — Business layer basics (done)
Office screen with P&L, consumables that drain in the microscope, parts stock (new + salvaged), supplier orders (next day) and rush buys, rent/electricity/advertising, customer queue with walk-outs, warranty comebacks, marketplace flips (previous repairs, missing parts, unrepairable carbonised boards → scrap for parts; programmed MCU only from donors).

## ✅ Milestone 3b — Equipment, devices, market depth (done)
- Rework kit: iron tips, hot-air nozzles, wick widths, flux grades (rosin → no-clean → premium), low-melt alloy for plastic connectors; brush wear, wipes and IPA consumed per use
- Five more devices with tiered faults: BoomPuck speaker, ViewMax 24 monitor power board, Sbox One X power board, Button handheld, Funstation 5 HDMI/APU board (retimer, HDMI port, ESD, HPD)
- Plug-in modules (battery, screen, speaker, LED strip): unplug / fit new or used, no soldering
- Devices unlock by reputation; market crazes (🔥 resale ×1.3–1.8 for a few days)
- Delivery services (economy 3 days / standard 2 / express next day); expensive chips, screens and batteries are special-order only (no rush buy)
- Store-room: parts-only listings and given-up projects become donors; pull parts one at a time (tested on removal)

## ✅ Milestone 3c — Diagnostics & customer options (done)
- Instruments (Equipment tab): ESR meter (meter mode), oscilloscope (Scope tool 7: ripple, switching, data/audio presence), thermal camera (auto-ranged heat overlay, T)
- PSU probes for voltage injection; new faults: dried-out caps (high ESR → regulator sag + ripple), MOSFETs that won't switch, open inductors, dead level shifter (Funstation HPD)
- Customer calls: next-day parts the customer pays for, waits for parts/donors (no walk-outs while agreed), buy dead devices as spares; online donor search with shipping
- Wick is consumed per joint (turns silver, cut with C); leaving the microscope keeps the work; slower brush wear; crooked parts visibly twisted under the microscope

## Milestone 3d — Business, next
- Expedited jobs, quotes the player sets, customer negotiation
- More tools that unlock capabilities (thermal camera, scope); reviews, specialization
